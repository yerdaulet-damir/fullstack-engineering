# Acceptance

- `npm start` serves the page and static assets on port `3001` by default.
- The document has a skip link, one `main`, a labelled signup form, and distinct benefits and FAQ sections.
- The layout remains usable at narrow widths and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Invalid or duplicate submissions show a useful inline message.
- A valid submission updates the page without navigating or reloading.
- `npm test` passes after the exercises are complete.
